Word highlight, 150ms per handover

The highlight follows the voice’s own per-character timings, not a guessed interval. It is a position, not a verdict, so it takes the lilac panel and never a score colour.

Under reduced motion the colour change is instant and the word is still marked — the 150ms transition only softens the handover and carries nothing.

Padding is box-shadow, not padding: horizontal padding on an inline word would shuffle the rest of the line as the voice moved through it.

Next, your microphone

Scoring a sound means hearing you make it. There is no way around the microphone, so here is exactly what happens.

  • 1Each take is recorded on this device and uploaded to be scored. Nothing is uploaded until you speak.
  • 2Takes are kept for 90 days and then deleted. Your sound history — the numbers, not the audio — is never expired on a timer.
  • 3When diagnostics are switched on by whoever runs this service, a copy of a recording can also be written to their disk. So we will not tell you nothing is ever stored.
  • 4You can export everything, or erase all of it, from the You tab — with the counts of what was deleted.

Three things happen here

The banner — 350ms rise plus one 420ms pop, and the pop is reserved for a first try or a personal best so it does not wear out on every pass.

The gain — the +22 wipes in at 420ms as a clip, not a slide: the row did not move, so nothing should look like it did.

The playing syllable — a ring in a different channel from the band colour, because the band colour is the score and must keep saying that. Reduced motion leaves the static ring, which is the part carrying the meaning.
Motion, as one decision

One curve — cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) — and three durations. Everything below is stripped by the blanket reduced-motion rule, so each row states what remains.

150msTaps, chips, word handover, hover tints. Static: the colour or scale it lands on.
350msScreen arrival, 12px and a fade, one direction, no exit. Static: the screen, already there.
500msA pass, once — and the brand rule drawing in under the header. Static: the banner and the rule.
loopOnly three: the live meter, the mic-is-open pulse, the playing syllable. Each has a static twin in the same box.

Every animation here is transform or opacity. Nothing animates height, width or a background position, so no frame of this flow costs a layout pass — which is what “smooth” actually means on the device this is tested on.

Responsive, as one decision

Drag the preview narrow and wide — this shell is the same markup at 360 and 1280. Nothing here is a fixed-width frame.

Two things deliberately do not scale: the 48px tap floor, which is a minimum and not a proportion, and the 16.5px input size, which is the iOS zoom floor. A fluid type scale that dips under either one breaks the product on the device it matters on.
