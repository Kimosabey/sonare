Five, chosen deliberately. Anything older falls back to the browser default rather than joining a list of forty nobody audits.

Android needs none of this — it generates the splash from the manifest. background_color: #fcfbfe (the page ground), theme_color: #41009a (OS chrome). Getting those two the wrong way round is the flash.

Maskable icon: 20% safe zone, art inside the circle. Not declared maskable until this padded version exists — today’s 512×512 was not drawn for it.
