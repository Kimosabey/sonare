Today states the shape of the sitting before the learner commits, and the streak counts attendance only. The Journey is a spine, never a locked path — every lesson is openable, and each unit carries a can-do outcome with its receipts beside it. Progress shows sounds and coverage on one screen, with “not enough history yet” as a real state. Nothing on these screens attaches a number to the person: no points, no XP, no league, no ranking.
